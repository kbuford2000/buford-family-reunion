Our Story

Where It All Began

The Buford family is a family bound not just by blood, but by a deep and enduring commitment to one another. Through the years, through moves and marriages, through joy and loss, through cities and states — the Buford name has carried with it something irreplaceable: a sense of home wherever family gathers.

What began as a small family tradition has grown into something that spans generations. Children who came to early reunions in their parents' arms now bring their own little ones. The family that once fit around a single table now fills entire hotel floors and beaches. Yet something essential has never changed — that feeling of belonging, of being known, of being loved without condition.

From Panama City to Gatlinburg, from Orange Beach to Galveston to Virginia Beach, and now on to Detroit — every reunion adds a new chapter to our shared story. This page is where we preserve it.
