🏙️ Detroit 2027 · July 8–12

Something for Everyone
in the Motor City

Big family, different speeds — kids who need to run, grandparents who'd rather sit by the river, night owls, and first-time explorers. Here are ideas for each, so no one gets left out.

🗓️ We're a year out — these are ideas we're exploring, not locked in

This is a living list. As we get closer to July 2027 we'll research, add, and swap suggestions based on what the family wants to do — and what's open, priced right, and easy for big groups. See something you love (or something missing)? Tell us below.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
For the little ones

Families with Kids

🔬Michigan Science Center

Hands-on STEM exhibits and a planetarium — indoors, so it's a perfect hot-day or rainy-day backup.

🦁Detroit Zoo

125 acres and 2,000+ animals, fully stroller-friendly. Easy to split off and regroup.

🚂The Henry Ford + Greenfield Village

Living-history village with Model T rides and a steam train — an easy full day for all ages.

🌳Belle Isle

Aquarium, conservatory, nature center, giant slide, and a calm beach — all on one island park.

🎠Riverwalk + Campus Martius

Riverfront carousel, splash fountain, and free outdoor movie nights downtown.

⚾Tigers Game at Comerica Park

There's a Ferris wheel and carousel inside the ballpark — a game doubles as a kids' outing.

💛
Comfortable & easy pace

Family Who Aren't as Mobile

🚶🏾Detroit Riverwalk

Smooth paved path, benches, shade, and accessible restrooms and picnic areas — a gentle stroll or roll.

🖼️Detroit Institute of Arts

Elevators and loaner wheelchairs; the Diego Rivera murals are a sit-and-take-it-in highlight.

🚗The Henry Ford Museum

Flat, wide aisles, with wheelchairs and scooters to rent on site.

🛥️Detroit River Cruise

Seated, scenic, no walking — a great equalizer where every age can be together.

🚝People Mover & QLine

Fully accessible downtown transit; the elevated People Mover loop is a low-effort sightseeing ride in itself.

⚾Comerica Park

Ramps and elevators throughout — the whole family can sit together for a Tigers game.

🍸
After the kids are down

Young Professionals & Night Owls

🌆3Fifty Terrace

Rooftop atop Music Hall — skyline views, a DJ, and 10,000 sq ft of open-air space downtown.

🍹I|O Godfrey Rooftop

Corktown rooftop with a retractable glass roof, cocktails, and small plates.

🎧Exodos Rooftop

Multi-level Greektown lounge known for local DJs and live music.

🎸Tin Roof

Live-music bar with a rooftop terrace overlooking Comerica Park.

🏛️Greektown

Walkable casino, late-night eats, and bars all in one district — easy to meet up and split off.

🗺️
See something new

Explorers & First-Timers

🎶Motown Museum (Hitsville U.S.A.)

Stand in Studio A where the hits were recorded — the quintessential Detroit experience.

✊🏾Charles H. Wright Museum

One of the world's largest museums of African American history — especially meaningful for a family reunion.

🖼️Detroit Institute of Arts

Home of the awe-inspiring Diego Rivera Detroit Industry murals.

🏭Ford Rouge Factory Tour

Watch F-150s roll off the line — a genuine "wow." Or the Piquette Ave Plant, the original Model T factory.

🎨Riverwalk, Dequindre Cut & Pewabic Pottery

Art-lined riverfront greenway plus a working National Historic Landmark pottery studio.

🎉
The whole family, together

Events for Everyone

⚾Tigers Game (Group Block)

Reserve a block of seats — something for every age and mobility level in one outing.

🛳️Private River Cruise

Charter a boat (Diamond Jack's, ~100 people) or the Detroit Princess dinner cruise — everyone in one place, scenic and seated.

🍖Riverfront Cookout

Keep the cookout tradition going at a Belle Isle or Riverwalk picnic area — Detroit style.

🏙️Reunion Banquet Venue

Riverfront halls like Waterview Loft at Port Detroit or the Roostertail — or the Marriott RenCen right at our hotel block.

🤝Free Reunion Planning Help

Visit Detroit offers a free family-reunion service — group hotel blocks, venue matching, and discounts. Worth a call early.

💡 Have an idea for Detroit?

A restaurant, a landmark, a show, a can't-miss for the kids — send it our way and we'll add it to the list as we plan the 2027 reunion.
